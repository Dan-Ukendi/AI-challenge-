      On the map

      Thick outline = the commune. Blue = observed flood. Orange dots = buildings in or near the flood. Coloured dots = sites listed in OpenStreetMap that were in or within 100 m of the flood. Blue pins with a name = the nearest communes with lower modelled exposure (planning candidates, not an evacuation order).

      Priority list

      Belgian communes in the mapped zones, ranked by the number of residents who lived in the flooded area.

      Priority levels compare communes across Belgium, Germany and the Netherlands together, so they are relative, not official limits. In Belgium no commune reaches "highest priority", because Belgium has only two mapped zones and the German floods were much larger. Click a row to open the commune.

      Sites near the flood

      Sites listed in OpenStreetMap that were inside the observed flood or within 100 m of it. This does not mean they were damaged.
