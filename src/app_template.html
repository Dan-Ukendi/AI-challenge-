      On the map

      Thick outline = the commune. Blue = observed flood. Orange dots = buildings in or near the flood. Coloured dots = sites listed in OpenStreetMap that were in or within 100 m of the flood. Blue pins with a name = the nearest communes with lower modelled exposure (planning candidates, not an evacuation order).

      My route

      Drop a pin on the map (or use your position): we plan the roads to the nearest shelters that avoid the water in our data.

      Planning aid, not a navigation or a warning. Our principle (a team choice): the flood data is the safe side. A road outside it is treated as passable even in a flood this large; roads inside it are avoided. Real floods can differ, roads can be closed, and shelters may be closed or full. Never drive through flooded roads. In a real flood, follow the authorities.

        Live water levels

        Current water levels published by official services in the areas hit in July 2021. Each number is shown exactly as the source gives it.

        Do not compare heights between stations. Each gauge measures from its own reference point (its own zero, sea level NAP, or an altitude), so thousands of cm above sea level in Maastricht is not "more water" than half a metre above the gauge zero in Verviers. Colours appear only where the source publishes its own alert thresholds (North Rhine-Westphalia).
